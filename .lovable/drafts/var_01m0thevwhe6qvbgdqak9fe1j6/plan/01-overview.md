# A calmer, more caring Today

Today already runs on the real planner engine (same timeline, same drag-and-drop, same day-part bands as `/planner`), so the work here is not a rebuild — it's calming the surface, filling the caregiver-shaped gaps, and making every card do something when you touch it.

## What's off right now

- **Too many entry points at once.** Header, demo banner, check-in prompt, quick-add bar, capacity hints, overdue, habit nudge, period tabs, tray button and two rails all stack above the grid before you see your day.
- **Care is a name list.** The People card shows avatars and a "kind" label — no per-person tasks, meds, appointments or notes. For a caregiver this is the most important card and currently the least useful.
- **Meals are missing on Today.** The planner has a meal lane; Today has no breakfast/lunch/dinner surface at all.
- **Self-care and journal are buried.** Journal sits inside the Grow column at the bottom; the exhale prompt is the last thing on the page.
- **Cards look calm but act static.** Most are read-only with an "All →" link; you have to leave Today to do anything.
- **Mobile plan tab is deep.** Two collapsible sections, each with its own scroller, nested in the page scroller.

## The shape it should take

One page, three calm zones, same on both breakpoints — just stacked vs. side by side.

1. **Arrive** — greeting, weather/moon, capacity, one anchor, one intention. Quiet, wide, unhurried.
2. **The day** — planner timeline as the hero, with meals inline in the lane and a now-marker.
3. **The circle** — per-person care, home, routines & habits, self-care, journal, close-the-day. Collapsible, remembered.
