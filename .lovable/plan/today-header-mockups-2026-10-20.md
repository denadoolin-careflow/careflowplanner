# Today header mockups

Make 3–4 visual mockups of a new Today header for you to compare. The app won't change until you pick one.

## What every option shows
- Today's date (weekday, month, day)
- Weather (temperature and conditions)
- Season (for example "Fall · Apple Orchard")
- Moon phase and sign (for example "Waxing Crescent in Sagittarius")
- Cycle phase (only shows if cycle tracking is on)
- Current rhythm phase: Plan, Grow, Care or Let go, in its own phase color
- A short affirmation for the day

## Options
1. **Calm band.** One slim cream strip. The date sits on the left in the serif heading font. Small chips on the right show weather, season, moon, cycle and phase. The affirmation sits in italics underneath.
2. **Moon card.** A rounded card with a large moon picture on the left. On the right: the date, the moon sign, and a 4-step Plan → Grow → Care → Let go track with today's phase highlighted. Weather and season go in the corner. The affirmation goes along the bottom.
3. **Botanical greeting.** A soft seasonal picture strip in the background, with the date and the affirmation on top. A row of small icon pills shows weather, season, moon, cycle and phase.
4. **Compact phone stack.** Made for phones. The date and weather share the top line, with swipeable chips below. The affirmation is in a tappable card that can be collapsed.

## Delivery
- Each option is drawn in CareFlow colors: cream, burgundy, forest green, olive and gold. Plan, Grow, Care and Let go use their own phase colors.
- I'll show the options side by side for you to choose from. Building the one you pick into Today is a separate step.

## Technical notes
- Mockups are made as images only. No app code changes.
- The live version would later reuse the existing moon, cycle, season and rhythm data.
